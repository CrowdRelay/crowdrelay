# Product specification

CrowdRelay converts anonymous attention into permission-based fan relationships and measurable live demand.

## Virya reward model

- Guest-list places are event-specific draws based on explicit interest in that event.
- Physical albums are currently one global draw with three winners, not three albums per concert.
- A verified concert check-in contributes to the global album draw during its configured open/close window.
- Referrals and check-ins increase entries subject to the draw cap.
- Physical fulfillment happens outside CrowdRelay; CrowdRelay does not store shipping addresses.

## Staff QR requirements

The operator must be able to select a published concert, set a bounded activation window and optional cap, generate SVG/PNG/A4 output, display full-screen, inspect check-in count and revoke the campaign immediately.
