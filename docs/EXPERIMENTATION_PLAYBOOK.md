# VIRYA Experimentation Playbook

CrowdRelay experiments optimize aggregate product and show outcomes without profiling people. Assignment stays deterministic and stable; observations remain aggregate first-party facts.

## Allowed

- CTA wording/order and non-deceptive presentation.
- Timing of non-essential fan messages within policy and quiet-hours rules.
- Ordering of Home, Afterglow, Synesthesia and show-growth surfaces.
- Aggregate show-growth tactics where ticket, attendance, click or revenue outcomes are measured.

## Never experiment on

- consent, privacy, account deletion, security, authentication or refund rights;
- accessibility or safety-critical staff flows;
- essential ticket/operational notifications;
- individualized or discriminatory ticket pricing;
- names, emails, phones, precise locations, health/sensitive attributes or inferred sensitive categories.

## Shipping guardrail

Every experiment needs a primary metric, a guardrail metric, a minimum sample, bounded exploration, an explicit stop condition and a rollback path. A variant may graduate only from aggregate observations; an LLM may summarize results but may not assign people or override the deterministic decision rule.
