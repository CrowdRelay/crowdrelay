# Legal and privacy notes

This repository provides technical controls, not legal advice. Before enabling a promotion, publish clear rules covering organizer identity, eligibility, dates, prize inventory, selection method, notification, claim deadline, disqualification and data use.

Collect only data required for the stated purpose. Keep marketing consent separate from participation where required. Concert check-ins are attendance evidence and should have an explicit retention period. Do not place email addresses, session identifiers or personal data in QR payloads, URLs, logs or webhook metadata.

The global three-album campaign and each event guest-list campaign should be reviewed as separate promotional actions even when they share the same fan account and weighting engine.
