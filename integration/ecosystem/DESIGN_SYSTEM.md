# Virya ecosystem design contract

The products belong to one family without sharing one UI runtime. Tokens describe semantic roles, not a requirement that every screen look identical.

- **Virya** is editorial/public. Space and content lead; signal yellow marks interaction, focus and selected state.
- **Virya Signal** is utilitarian. Fan mode stays calm; Crew/Show Mode may be denser but keeps 44 px controls and a 12 px minimum micro type size.
- **Synesthesia** is expressive. Room art and sensory motion can use red/cyan freely; after reveal the HUD yields to the artwork. Reduced motion remains a first-class readable experience.
- **CrowdRelay/ViryaOS** is operational. Status colors communicate state and timelines expose metadata only.

Cross-product continuity is participation, not points: tickets/passes, AREA discoveries and Synesthesia completion are shown as a fan's history. Do not introduce XP, levels, streak pressure or currencies merely for engagement.
